# Support channels

Email support is available Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC, excluding
published company holidays. Contact support@northwind.example and include your workspace
ID. Never include passwords, API secrets or customer financial data in a support ticket.

Pro has a target first response of 2 business days. Team has a target first response
of 4 business hours. Free customers use the community forum and have no response-time
commitment. Target first response is not a guarantee of issue resolution time.

Team workspace owners can flag a production outage as priority one. The incident team
provides updates through the public status page every 30 minutes during an active
priority-one outage. Sales questions go to sales@northwind.example.
