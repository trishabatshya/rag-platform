# Free plan

The Free plan costs 0 USD and includes 2 member seats, 1 GB of document storage and
10 scheduled automation runs per month. A Free workspace can have 2 active projects.
The Free plan has no contractual uptime SLA and support is provided through the public
community forum. A credit card is not required to create a Free workspace.

Free workspaces that exceed their storage allowance become unable to upload new
documents. Existing documents remain readable and exportable. Deleting documents
or upgrading restores uploads. Automation runs stop when the monthly allowance
is exhausted and resume on the first day of the next calendar month.

Free workspaces are never automatically upgraded or charged. Only an owner can start
a paid subscription. The Pro and Team plans are documented separately.
