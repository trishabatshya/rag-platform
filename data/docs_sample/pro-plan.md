# Pro plan

The Pro plan costs 19 USD per workspace per month. Pro includes 5 member seats, 20 GB of
document storage and 100 scheduled automation runs per month. Pro supports one workspace
owner and up to four additional members. Unused automation runs do not roll over.

Pro includes email support with a target first response of 2 business days. Pro does not
include SAML single sign-on, a contractual uptime SLA or priority incident support. API
access is included, with the limits described in the rate limits document.

Extra seats cannot be purchased individually on Pro. Upgrade to Team when a workspace
needs more than 5 members. Storage overages cost 0.10 USD per GB per month and appear on
the following invoice. A Pro workspace can have up to 10 active projects.
