# Team uptime service level agreement

The Team plan guarantees 99.9 percent monthly uptime for the document API. Scheduled
maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance is excluded. The Free and Pro plans
do not include a contractual SLA. The status page publishes measured availability.

If eligible monthly uptime is below 99.9 percent but at least 99.0 percent, the service
credit is 10 percent of that month's subscription charge. Below 99.0 percent, the service
credit is 25 percent. Credits do not apply to usage-based storage charges.

An owner must request a credit within 30 calendar days of the affected month's end.
Submit the workspace ID and incident dates to support@northwind.example. Service credits
are applied to a future invoice and are not paid as cash refunds.
