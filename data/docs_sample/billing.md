# Billing and invoices

Northwind Cloud invoices are issued on the first day of each calendar month in USD.
Invoices are payable within 15 calendar days. Workspace owners can download PDF invoices
from Settings > Billing > Invoices. Billing contacts receive an email copy of each invoice.

Only a workspace owner can change the billing contact or payment method. Supported
payment methods are Visa, Mastercard and bank transfer. A failed card payment is retried
after 3 days and again after 7 days. A workspace becomes read-only after 14 days of
nonpayment; documents remain available for export.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remaining days in the month.
Downgrades take effect at the next monthly renewal. Taxes appear as separate invoice
line items and depend on the customer's billing address.
