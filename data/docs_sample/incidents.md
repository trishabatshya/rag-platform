# Incident communications

The public status page is status.northwind.example. The incident team publishes an
initial notification within 15 minutes of confirming a production outage. During
an active priority-one incident, updates are published every 30 minutes.

A post-incident review is published within 5 business days after resolution. Reviews
include the timeline, impact and prevention actions. Customer names and document
contents are excluded from public incident reports. Team owners can subscribe to
incident notifications from Settings > Notifications.

The status page covers the document API, web console and automation scheduler separately.
An incident notification does not automatically create an SLA credit request. Owners
must request credits using the process described in the SLA document.
