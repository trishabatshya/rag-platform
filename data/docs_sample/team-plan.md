# Team plan

The Team plan costs 79 USD per workspace per month. Team includes 25 member seats, 100 GB
of document storage and 1000 scheduled automation runs per month. Team supports multiple
workspace owners. Unused automation runs do not roll over.

Team includes SAML single sign-on, priority email support and a contractual 99.9 percent
monthly uptime SLA. The target first support response is 4 business hours. Team allows
up to 100 active projects and supports a dedicated billing contact.

Extra seats cannot be purchased individually on Team. Workspaces needing more than
25 members must contact sales for an Enterprise agreement. Storage overages cost
0.08 USD per GB per month and appear on the following invoice.
