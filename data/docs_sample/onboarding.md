# Onboarding a workspace

To onboard, verify your email address, choose a workspace name, select a hosting region,
and invite teammates. The onboarding checklist has four steps. A workspace initially uses
the Free plan unless its owner explicitly selects a paid plan.

Invitations expire after 72 hours. Only workspace owners can send or revoke invitations.
An invited user must verify their email address before joining. The default member role
is viewer; an owner can change it to editor after the invitation is accepted.

Import Markdown or plain-text documents from the Documents > Import screen. A single
import accepts up to 100 files. The welcome project can be deleted after your own first
project has been created. The console shows checklist completion to workspace owners.
