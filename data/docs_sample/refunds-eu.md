# European Union refund policy

Customers with an EU billing address may request a subscription refund within 14 calendar
days of their first paid subscription purchase. The EU refund window is 14 calendar days.
Renewal invoices and usage-based storage overages are not eligible for this refund.

Submit the invoice ID and workspace ID to billing@northwind.example. An EU refund request
does not require an explanation. Approved refunds return to the original payment method
within 5 business days. Cancelling a subscription alone does not submit a refund request.

The workspace remains readable until the end of the paid period unless the owner requests
immediate closure. This fictional policy is a corpus fixture, not consumer legal advice.
