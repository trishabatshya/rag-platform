# United States refund policy

Customers with a US billing address may request a subscription refund within 7 calendar
days of their first paid subscription purchase. The US refund window is 7 calendar days.
Renewal invoices and usage-based storage overages are not eligible for this refund.

Submit the invoice ID and workspace ID to billing@northwind.example and describe the
reason for the request. Approved refunds return to the original payment method within
10 business days. Cancelling a subscription alone does not submit a refund request.

The workspace remains readable until the end of the paid period unless the owner requests
immediate closure. The EU policy is documented separately and uses a different window.
